    Vulnerability checks

    WPScout can check the plugins, theme and WordPress version it finds against databases of known vulnerabilities. Add a key for any of these. Results from each are combined, and Chrome asks to let WPScout reach each provider you turn on.

    Wordfence

    Free key. Get a key at wordfence.com

    Downloads the full list at most once a day and checks versions on your computer. The sites you scan are never sent to Wordfence.

    WPScan

    Free key for non-commercial use: 25 requests a day. Commercial use needs a paid plan. See wpscan.com

    Checks run when you open the popup, one request per plugin, theme and WordPress version, so a free key covers about two sites a day. Plugin and theme names (not their versions) are sent to WPScan, and results aren’t stored.

    Vulnerability data provided by Wordfence Intelligence and WPScan, depending on the keys you add.
